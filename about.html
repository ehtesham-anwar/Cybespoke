About

A small studio that gets things done.

We help UK and Irish businesses use AI safely. We build AI made for your business (on your own computers or online), test your systems for security holes before hackers find them, and send engineers to work inside your team. Everything we build is yours at the end.

How we work

Five steps. No surprises.

Every project follows the same five steps. You always know what is happening and what it costs.

  1. Phase one

    Assess

    We learn about you first.

    We look at your business, your data and your risks. You get a written plan and a fixed price, so you know the cost before we start.

    • A chat with your team
    • What "success" looks like
    • Written plan & fixed price
  2. Phase two

    Pilot

    One real task, working in weeks.

    We pick one real job and make it work, usually in two to four weeks. You see it helping before you spend more.

    • One real task, live
    • Weekly demos
    • You decide what comes next
  3. Phase three

    Secure

    We check it is safe.

    Before more people use it, our security team tries to break it. We add safety rules and make sure only the right people can get in.

    • We try to hack it (safely)
    • Safety rules for the AI
    • Right access for the right people
  4. Phase four

    Roll out

    Your whole team gets it.

    We connect it to the tools you already use, add more users and tasks, and train your team.

    • Connected to your tools
    • Training for your team
    • Clear how‑to guides
  5. Phase five

    Run

    We keep it working.

    We watch it day and night, update it and check its security, all for one flat monthly fee.

    • Watched 24/7
    • Updates & security checks
    • Monthly report in plain English
