Common
questions.

The honest answers

Are open models as good as ChatGPT?

For most business tasks — summaries, search, drafting, sorting — modern open models are very close. For the hardest tasks we can route to a trusted cloud model, with your approval.

Do we need to buy GPUs?

Not always. Many teams start in their own private cloud and pay by the hour. We only suggest hardware when the numbers make sense.

Where does our data go?

Only where you decide. On‑prem and private cloud set‑ups keep it inside your systems. If you use public inference, we pick providers with no data retention and UK/EU hosting.

Is it GDPR friendly?

Yes — keeping data in your own systems makes GDPR much simpler. We help with the data protection impact assessment (DPIA) too.

Who owns it?

You do. The servers, accounts, code and any fine‑tuned models are all yours.
