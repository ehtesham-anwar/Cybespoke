Apps people actually keep

iPhone and Android apps that feel polished, work offline when the signal drops, and welcome people in without losing them. Native iOS, native Android, or one cross‑platform codebase — we pick what fits.

  • First impressions

    The first 60 seconds matter most. We design sign‑up and welcome flows that don't lose people.

    Apple Pay, Google Pay, free trials, paywalls and receipts — handled cleanly.

  • Works offline

    Saves your work locally and syncs when signal comes back. Works on the train, in the lift, on the Tube.

    Targeted push messages and clever links that open the right screen. Respectful by default.

B

CRMs & internal tools

Off‑the‑shelf CRMs always need workarounds. We build the CRM, the ops dashboard, the back‑office tool — whatever you need — to fit the way your business actually runs.

  • Customer 360

    One screen with the contact, the deals, the notes, the support tickets — no more swivel‑chairing between five tools.

  • Custom pipelines

    Define the stages your team actually uses. Drag, drop, automate.

  • Role‑based access

    Sales sees deals, accounts sees invoices, support sees tickets. Everyone gets exactly the data they need.

  • Integrations

    Plug in your accounting, email, calendar, phone system. Two‑way sync, not one‑off CSV exports.

  • Live reporting

    Dashboards your board will actually read. KPIs, leaderboards, weekly digests — emailed on Monday morning.

  • GDPR & audit logs

    Encryption at rest, right‑to‑forget workflows, immutable audit logs — the boring stuff, done right.
