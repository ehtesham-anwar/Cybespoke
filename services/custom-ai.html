Common questions

Straight answers.

What is the "safety layer" (harness)?

It is the software around the AI. Think of the AI as a brain: the safety layer gives it the right information, the right tools and clear limits, and keeps a record of what it does.

AI on our own computers, or online: which is better?

It depends on the job. AI on your own computers keeps private information with you. Online AI is often smarter and cheaper to start with. Many clients use both, and the safety layer picks the right one each time.

Will you change our current software, or build something new?

Whatever suits you. If your current software works well, we add AI to it. If not, we build a new web or phone app that connects to your information.

How do you make the AI bill smaller?

AI companies charge by the word (tokens). We keep instructions short, send only what is needed, use cheaper AI for easy jobs and reuse saved answers. Same results, smaller bill.

Do we need to buy expensive computers?

Not always. Many teams start with online AI, or rent computers by the hour in their own cloud. We only suggest buying when the numbers make sense.

Are we stuck with one AI company?

No. Our setup works with many AIs, so you can switch when a better or cheaper one comes out.

Where does our information go?

Only where you decide. Private information can stay on your own computers. For online AI, we pick companies that do not keep your data, with UK or EU servers where possible.

Who owns it?

You do. The code, the computers, the accounts, the instructions and any AI we train are all yours.
