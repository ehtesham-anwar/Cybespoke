Common
questions.

The honest answers

What is an AI harness?

It's the software around an AI model. The model is the brain; the harness gives it the right information, the right tools and clear limits — and records what it does.

Local or cloud models — which is better?

It depends on the task. Local models keep sensitive data in your systems. Cloud models are often stronger and cheaper to start. Many clients use both, and the harness picks the right one for each job.

Will you change our current system, or build something new?

Whatever suits you. If your current software works, we add AI to it. If not, we build a separate web or mobile app that connects to your data.

How do you cut token use?

Tokens are what AI companies charge for. We keep prompts short, send only the data needed, use cheaper models for easy jobs and cache repeat answers. Same results, smaller bill.

Do we need to buy GPUs?

Not always. Many teams start with cloud models or their own private cloud and pay by the hour. We only suggest hardware when the numbers make sense.

Are we locked into one AI company?

No. The harness works with many models, so you can switch when a better or cheaper one comes out.

Where does our data go?

Only where you decide. Sensitive data can stay on local models. For cloud models, we pick providers with no data retention and UK/EU hosting where possible.

Who owns it?

You do. The code, servers, accounts, prompts and any fine‑tuned models are all yours.
