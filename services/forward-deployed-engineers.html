A

What a forward deployed engineer does

Not a ticket queue. Not a slide deck. An engineer who sits with your people — in person or online — finds the problems worth solving, and builds the fix with you.

  • Find the real problems

    They talk to your team, watch how work gets done, and spot what slows you down.

  • Build AI in your stack

    AI custom software, tools and integrations, wired into your real systems.

  • Build & ship

    Working software in days, not months — shipped, tested and used.

  • Connect your systems

    Your CRM, finance, files and email — joined up so data flows.

  • Secure as they go

    Every change follows our security checklist, backed by our security team.

  • Train your team

    They leave your people able to run, change and grow what was built.

B

A good fit if…

Forward deployed engineering works best when you know there is a problem, but not yet the exact solution.

  • You want AI but don't know where to start

    We find the two or three uses that pay back, and build those first.

  • You have no in‑house engineers

    Get senior skills without a long, costly hire.

  • Your team is stretched

    Add a senior pair of hands who needs very little hand‑holding.

  • Your data is sensitive

    Someone who builds with privacy and security from day one.

  • Your tools don't talk to each other

    Someone to join them up, properly.

  • You've been burned by agencies

    Weekly demos and real code you own — no black boxes.

Common
questions.

The honest answers

What does "forward deployed" mean?

It means the engineer works inside your business, close to your people and problems — not far away in an agency, waiting for tickets.

On‑site or remote?

Both. On‑site in Belfast and Northern Ireland, and remote with regular visits across the UK and Ireland.

How is this different from a contractor?

You get a senior engineer backed by our whole studio — AI, security and development experts — not one person on their own.

Is there a minimum term?

A two‑week sprint is the smallest. Embedded plans run month to month with 30 days' notice.

Who owns the work?

You do. All code, docs and accounts are yours from day one.
