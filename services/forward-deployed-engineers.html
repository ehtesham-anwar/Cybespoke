Common questions

Straight answers.

What does "forward deployed" mean?

It means the engineer works inside your business, close to your people and problems, not far away in an agency waiting for tasks.

In our office, or online?

Both. In person in Belfast and Northern Ireland, and online with regular visits across the UK and Ireland.

How is this different from a freelancer?

You get a senior engineer backed by our whole team of AI, security and software experts, not one person on their own.

Is there a minimum time?

A two‑week sprint is the smallest. "Part of your team" runs month by month, with 30 days' notice.

Who owns the work?

You do. All code, guides and accounts are yours from day one.
