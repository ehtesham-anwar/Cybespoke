Common
questions.

The honest answers

Will testing break our live systems?

We plan tests to avoid that, agree the timing with you, and can test on a staging copy first.

Do you certify us for Cyber Essentials or ISO 27001?

We prepare you and collect the evidence. The final certificate comes from an accredited body — we work alongside them.

Our AI comes from OpenAI or another vendor. Can you still test it?

Yes. Most AI risks are in how the model is connected to your data and tools — that is what we test.

How often should we test?

At least once a year, and after any big change, like a new app, a new AI feature or a cloud move.

Can you fix what you find?

Yes. We can fix it ourselves, or one of our forward deployed engineers can work with your team.
