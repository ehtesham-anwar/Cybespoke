AI security

Keep your AI from being tricked.

AI brings new risks. People can trick a chatbot, make it leak private data, or get an AI helper to do things it shouldn't. We test for these and add safety rules.

Pass safety checks

Common questions

Straight answers.

Will testing break our live systems?

We plan tests to avoid that, agree the timing with you, and can test on a copy first.

Do you give us the Cyber Essentials or ISO 27001 certificate?

We get you ready and collect the evidence. The certificate itself comes from an approved checker, and we work alongside them.

Our AI comes from OpenAI or another company. Can you still test it?

Yes. Most risks are in how the AI is connected to your information and tools. That is what we test.

How often should we test?

At least once a year, and after any big change, like a new app, a new AI feature or moving to the cloud.

Can you fix what you find?

Yes. We can fix it ourselves, or one of our engineers can work with your team.
