How it goes

From first chat to launch day.

  1. 01

    Get to know you

    A week of discovery

    We listen, ask the awkward questions, and map out the shortest route to something useful. You leave with a written plan and a clear price.

  2. 02

    Design it

    Real screens, not just pictures

    The look, the words, and the way it moves — designed and shown to you on a working preview, not just static slides.

  3. 03

    Build it

    Two‑week build cycles

    Live preview from week one. A demo every Friday. You always know what's been done and what's next.

  4. 04

    Launch & handover

    Go live with confidence

    A speed check, accessibility check, analytics, monitoring and short docs for your team. Optional ongoing support if you want us to stick around.
